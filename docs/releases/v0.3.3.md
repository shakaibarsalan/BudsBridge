**The buds you just released stay "the buds".** If you hadn't picked a hotkey target, releasing
the buds from the dock moved the dock's **Buds** tile to whichever device came first by name, and
`Ctrl+Alt+B` then connected that device, maybe a speaker, instead of your buds. Now the tile and the
hotkey stay on the device connected most recently, until you connect another one.

The README also has a 70-second walkthrough video with a voice-over, built from the app in demo mode
by `scripts/video/make.sh`.

The build isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen warns once: **More info → Run anyway**.

### Updating

Opening the new `BudsBridge.exe` while 0.3.1 or 0.3.2 runs switches to the new one by itself. From
0.3.0 or older, quit the old copy first (right-click the tray icon → **Quit**).

### Also in 0.3.2

Paired phones that Windows won't stream audio from are listed with the reason, and **Copy
diagnostics** in settings shows what Windows reports about them.

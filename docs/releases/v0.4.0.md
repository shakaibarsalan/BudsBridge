**BudsBridge for Mac.** The same dock now runs on macOS, from the menu bar: see your paired buds,
connect or release them with one click, toggle them from any app with **⌃⌥B** (Control+Option+B),
reconnect after a drop, and open at login. One download for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 12
Monterey or later.

### Phone audio on a Mac

macOS can't act as a Bluetooth speaker for a phone, so the Windows bridge (phone → laptop → buds
over Bluetooth) can't exist on a Mac. An iPhone or iPad gets the same result with **AirPlay**: play
something, tap AirPlay and choose the Mac, and it plays in your buds along with the Mac's own sound.
The dock explains this, and opens the AirPlay Receiver setting when it is off. Android phones can't
play through a Mac yet.

### Download

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `BudsBridge.exe` | Windows: single file, no installer. Download and open it |
| `BudsBridge-0.4.0-windows-x64-setup.exe` | Windows: installer for your user only, no admin prompt |
| `BudsBridge-0.4.0-macos-universal.dmg` | Mac: open it and drag BudsBridge into Applications |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums for all three |

**First open on a Mac.** The app isn't notarized by Apple yet (that needs a paid Apple Developer
ID), so the first time macOS says it can't verify it. On macOS 15 and later: click **Done**, open
**System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to BudsBridge.
On macOS 12–14: Control-click BudsBridge in Applications and choose **Open**. Then allow Bluetooth
when macOS asks: BudsBridge needs it to see and connect your buds. It lives in the menu bar, with
no Dock icon.

**Windows** works as before. The build isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns once: **More
info → Run anyway**.

### Please tell us how it goes on your Mac

The Mac version is new. Every change builds it, checks its signature and starts it on GitHub's Mac
machines, but it hasn't been tried with real buds on a real Mac yet. If connecting or releasing
doesn't work with yours, open **All settings → Copy diagnostics** and open an issue with it.

### Updating

Windows: opening the new `BudsBridge.exe` while 0.3.1 or newer runs switches to the new one by
itself.

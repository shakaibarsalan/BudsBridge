**The dock's picture, redrawn.** The phone, the laptop and the buds now look like the devices they
are: AirPods-style buds that slowly orbit each other while they're connected, a laptop whose screen
shows the sound as a moving wave, and light travelling along every live link. When two phones play
through the laptop, both are drawn, each with its own link.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|

### Updating

Opening the new `BudsBridge.exe` while 0.3.1 or newer runs switches to the new one by itself. From
0.3.0 or older, quit the old copy first (right-click the tray icon → **Quit**).
